All notable changes to Libraria: the app and the server, which share one version number. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Add notes under **Unreleased** as changes land, in `### Added`, `### Changed`, `### Fixed` or `### Removed`. `pnpm release` turns them into a version.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-01

### Added

- Reader for EPUB, PDF and comics (CBZ, CBR) that works fully offline, installable as a web app on phones, tablets and computers.
- Libraries that combine several sources: files on the device, a local folder, WebDAV, Google Drive, Dropbox and Libraria servers.
- Libraria server: a self-hosted community library with owner, curator and member roles, invites, reviewed submissions and offline downloads.
- End-to-end encrypted sync of progress, bookmarks and settings through a server you choose.
- EPUB page turns that slide or curl, and an option to turn pages by swiping.
- On Android, share books to the installed app from other apps.
- About page explaining the app, the server and what a web app is, plus a disclaimer on content rights.
- Version numbers shown in the app, with build details and this list of changes.
